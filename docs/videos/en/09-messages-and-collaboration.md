# 09 — Messages and collaboration

- **Working title:** Messages and collaboration
- **YouTube title:** Chat, group chat and teamwork on weave patterns | Textile Platform
- **YouTube description:** The Textile Platform has messages built in: direct chats with other users, a chat for every shared group, and help from support. We show the inbox, the unread counter and the privacy setting in your profile. Then we follow a complete exercise in a weaving guild: the teacher shares a pattern, a student clones it, changes it, shares her version back and asks a question in the group chat.
- **Target length:** about 7:30 (min:s)
- **Audience:** everyone, especially DB-WEAVE and JBead desktop users who want to work with others
- **Learning goals:**
  - Use the Messages inbox: unread counter, search, sorting, direct and group conversations.
  - Start a direct chat from a user's page (Send message) or from the search.
  - Delete your own messages.
  - Open the group chat from the group page.
  - Ask support for help (Help).
  - Block invitations and direct messages in the profile.
  - Run a complete teacher–student exercise: share, clone, modify, share back, discuss.
