# 06 — Beading: first steps

- **Working title:** Beading: first steps
- **YouTube title:** Bead crochet patterns in your browser: first steps with the JBead editor | Textile Platform
- **YouTube description:** What is a bead crochet rope, and how do you design one? In this video we open a bead pattern, explain the draft, corrected and simulation views and the bead list you thread from, and draw a first rope with pencil, fill and pipette. You'll also see how the repeat shrinks the bead list to a short sequence. Part 6 of the Textile Platform tutorial series at textile-platform.ch.
- **Target length:** about 7:15
- **Audience:** newcomers to bead crochet and to the bead editor
- **Learning goals:**
  - Understand what a bead crochet rope is and that the pattern width is the circumference (beads around).
  - Read the four views: draft view, corrected view, simulation view and report view (bead list); know that rows are counted from the bottom.
  - Draw with the pencil (click toggles, drag draws a line, Ctrl for straight/45° lines), use fill, pipette and Ctrl+click.
  - Choose colours in the palette (click, keys 0–9) and change a colour with a double-click.
  - Understand the repeat in the status bar and why the bead list only shows one repeat.
  - Scroll, zoom, undo, save and close.
