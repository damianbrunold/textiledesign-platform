# 08 — Sharing and groups

- **Working title:** Sharing and groups
- **YouTube title:** Share weave and bead patterns: public links, cloning and groups | Textile Platform
- **YouTube description:** Learn how to share your patterns on the Textile Platform. We make a pattern public, copy your public link, clone other people's patterns and organise your own patterns in personal groups. Then we create a shared group for a weaving guild, invite members with different roles, share patterns into the group and make the group public.
- **Target length:** about 7:55 (min:s)
- **Audience:** everyone, especially DB-WEAVE and JBead desktop users who want to share their work
- **Learning goals:**
  - Tell public and private patterns apart (🌐 / 🔒) and switch between them in the list and in the editor.
  - Find and copy your public link (Profile ▸ Public link).
  - Open other users' patterns read-only and clone them into your own patterns.
  - Explain the difference between a personal group and a shared group.
  - Create a shared group, invite users, and choose the right role (Owner, Writer, Reader).
  - Share a pattern into one or more groups, and create patterns directly inside a group.
  - Make a group public, and manage members (change role, remove, leave).
